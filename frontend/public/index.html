Check if your password has appeared in known data breaches using the HaveIBeenPwned API. Your password is never sent — only the first 5 chars of its SHA-1 hash are transmitted.

how_it_works
// k-anonymity explained
Your password is hashed with SHA-1 locally in the browser
Only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent to HIBP
HIBP returns all hashes starting with those 5 chars
Your browser checks if your full hash is in the list
Your actual password never leaves your device
